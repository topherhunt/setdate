SetDate

Batch-update creation dates on your photos.

Drop images or a folder, pick a new date or shift by an offset, and hit Apply. SetDate writes all three EXIF date fields (DateTimeOriginal, CreateDate, ModifyDate) plus the macOS filesystem creation date, so Finder, Photos, and every other app agree on when the image was taken.

Supports JPG, PNG, TIFF, HEIC, WebP, and common RAW formats (ARW, CR2, CR3, NEF, DNG, ORF, RAF, RW2).
